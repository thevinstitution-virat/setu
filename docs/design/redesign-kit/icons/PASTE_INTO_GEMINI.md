### Style lock

- **Rendering:** soft-gloss 3D, like premium quick-commerce app icons. Shapes are rounded and slightly chunky, with friendly, a little exaggerated proportions. The finish is smooth satin plastic, paper, glass or food, with soft highlights and gentle subsurface glow on food. The style is not photographic, not flat and not cartoon-outlined.
- **Packaging is generic.** **No real brands, no logos, no readable text, letters or numbers on any pack.** Show a label only as simple colour bands, a plain shape (leaf, drop, star, wheat ear) or a soft gradient.
- **Palette:** neutrals are white, warm cream and soft grey. The app has two looks, so accents follow where each icon is used:
  - **Shop-facing icons** (categories C, empty states E, and B01, B04, B05, B19): fresh green #3CBE4E.
  - **Staff icons** (the other B icons): deep teal #155651 and saffron #DB5C17.
  - **Everywhere:** money blue #2A4BA0 for money, marigold #F2B33D for offers, mint #3CC9A0 for checks and success.

  Use red #E5484D only where the brief says so. Each category keeps its own product colour (listed per icon).
- **Camera:** front three-quarter view, rotated about 20–30° and slightly above. The object is centred and fills about **78 % of the frame**, and nothing is cropped.
- **Light:** soft key light from the **top left**, gentle fill from the right, and a thin rim light.
- **Background:** **transparent PNG**. If that isn't possible, use **pure flat white #FFFFFF**.
- **Shadow:** **NO floor shadow, NO contact shadow, NO reflection, NO ground plane.** This is the most important rule.
- **Never include:** text, logos, watermarks, human faces, hands, outlines, frames, or background scenery.
- **Size:** **2048 × 2048** PNG, with the file name exactly as listed.
- **Consistency:** every icon must look like it comes from one set. It must have the same gloss, light direction, roundness and level of detail as the approved anchors.
